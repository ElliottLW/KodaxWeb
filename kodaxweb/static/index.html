Set up a new camera

        
Connects a camera in pairing mode to your Wi-Fi, without the Kodak cloud.

      

        

          
1. Put the camera in pairing mode

          
Power the camera on and hold the pairing/reset button until the LED flashes. It then broadcasts its own Wi-Fi network called KodakCam-…

          
2. Connect this PC to that network

          
Join KodakCam-… from Windows' Wi-Fi menu. Keep this page open; it's served from this PC so it keeps working.
